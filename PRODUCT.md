# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (confirmed by the client's consultant, 07/10/2026). No build step; deployable on any static host or re-integrable into the current WordPress/Divi site.

## Users

Women entrepreneurs (artisans, coaches, therapists, small e-shops), mostly within ~50 km of Bordeaux, often working alone. They arrive from Instagram or word of mouth, usually on a phone, wanting either to meet peers (Petits Dej Entr'Femmes), to get autonomous on Instagram, or to hand off admin chores.

## Product Purpose

V2M Secrétaire is the freelance business of Valérie Mollier (Instagram: @valeriedegommetagestion, @v2m.secretaire). The homepage must:
1. Put the current month's **Petit Dej Entr'Femmes** first (photos/video + registration link).
2. Sell the **Accompagnement Instagram 1:1** (4 weeks).
3. Keep the **Offre Reels « Be ready »**.
4. Link to her **Beacons** page.
5. Show client reviews and video testimonial excerpts from Instagram.

The "Community manager" offer is removed (client request). Admin services (INPI formalities, address change, adding an activity…) will be detailed later by the client.

## Positioning

Not a faceless virtual assistant: a Bordeaux-area secretary with 20 years of experience as an accounting secretary, who also hosts a monthly in-person women entrepreneurs' breakfast and coaches Instagram herself. Tagline in use: « Je dégomme votre gestion et votre com' ».
